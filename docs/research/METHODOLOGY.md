# Research methodology

## Scope and order

Research official claims before presenting them. Use MARA/MRSM for admission/programme evidence, KPM for national/PKSK information and Cambridge for qualifications/syllabuses. A source on an official domain still needs applicability: the English MARA FAQ cited in Gemini concerns 2020, while newer MyMRSM sources describe 2027. Some filenames and footer dates disagree with document covers; record that discrepancy instead of choosing a convenient date.

Each claim records an ID, supported proposition, publisher/title/URL, access date, shown publication/update date or its absence, applicable intake/cohort/syllabus-year and status. See `docs/research/07-sources-and-verification.md`. Statuses distinguish verified scoped facts, partial evidence, general educational advice, unverified, conflicting and historical information. An unverified claim is not necessarily false, but cannot configure an official simulator or establish eligibility.

## Outputs

Research files 01–07 cover Cambridge, admission, programme, selection distinctions, preparation, question-bank research and source verification. File 08 reviews the Gemini dossier's 30 material claim groups and selected corrections. File 09 records privacy/evidence handling for parent-provided candidate images, without transcribing child identity. These support rather than replace `full_prompt.md`.

## Access, copyright and privacy

Read only lawfully accessible material. A resource directory is not evidence that restricted School Support Hub content was read. Link to official papers/mark schemes/examiner reports; do not copy them into the original question bank. Distinguish editorial practice alignment from exact official syllabus mapping.

The public call-up checking portal is an official source for the identity of that service, not proof of an individual result. Do not submit a child's identity without specifically authorised transmission. Keep supplied slips and candidate details private, outside Git/static serving/memory. General app guides link out to official lookup rather than collecting identity numbers.

## Review cadence and remaining work

Recheck time-sensitive content before each intake and when sources change. Check Cambridge code/year/route before adding extension content or interpreting a past paper. Seek current primary notices for exact IGCSE selection format, fees, quotas, aid and offer/appeal conditions. Independent pedagogical moderation, selected-year objective-by-objective maps and a detailed official KSSM/SPM comparison remain follow-up research; the app's clearly labelled primary-foundation practice can operate while these are unresolved.
