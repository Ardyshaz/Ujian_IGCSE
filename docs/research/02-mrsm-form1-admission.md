# MRSM Form 1 admission research

Research/access date: 15 November 2026. Applicability: the official sources explicitly labelled **2027 intake**, not a perpetual admission policy. All claims below need rechecking before another cycle. This document supports the authorised local build; it does not identify the child's personal assessment invitation.

## Primary evidence and eligibility

**M-201 — verified, cycle-specific:** The [official MyMRSM entry page, S-M01](https://mrsm.mara.gov.my/mymrsm/) states Malaysian citizenship, citizenship of a biological parent, health/independence, stated birth windows, parent/guardian/guarantor blacklist conditions and priority for qualifying low-income applicants. For Form 1 2027 the stated birth interval is 2 January 2014–1 January 2015. It distinguishes KPM-registered school applicants using PKSK and unregistered private-school applicants using UKKM, with additional selection conditions for first-choice programmes. Consult the source for complete qualifications. The platform must not collect identity, medical, blacklist or household-finance records to decide eligibility.

**M-202 — verified application period:** MARA's FAQ cover says 2027 despite its filename `FAQ MYMRSM2026.pdf`. It gives an application window of 10 July–14 August 2026, linked Form 1/Form 4 application portals and an unregistered-private-school MyMRSM route. It also describes online-only processing and the importance of first-choice special-programme selection. [Official MARA FAQ, S-M02](https://mrsm.mara.gov.my/mymrsm/FAQ%20MYMRSM2026.pdf).

## Parent-facing process: general guidance, not a live application service

1. Identify the intake and correct registration/application route from current official documents.
2. Read current eligibility and programme information before choosing schools. Check any personal edge case directly with MARA rather than relying on automated eligibility claims here.
3. Submit the actual application through the official service, not this practice app. Store the acknowledgement privately.
4. Check official assessment invitations; confirm centre, date, arrival time, identification, permitted materials, access needs and any special-programme assessment.
5. Check outcomes and the actual offer's acceptance deadline. Do not rely on an assumed universal seven-day deadline or automatic fallback programme.
6. Discuss travel, boarding readiness, costs and support with the relevant school before accepting an offer.

Steps 1–6 are recommended family workflow; they do not create new MARA requirements or promise particular outcomes.
