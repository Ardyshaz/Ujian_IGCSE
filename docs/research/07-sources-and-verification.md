## Claim status policy

- **Verified:** a narrowly phrased claim supported by a read official source, limited to its named year/route. “Verified” does not mean personal eligibility or guaranteed future applicability.
- **Historical:** genuine official evidence for an earlier period; useful for understanding change, not active instructions.
- **Pending/qualified:** incomplete evidence, differing routes or a document date/version issue. Keep the limit beside the claim.
- **Guidance/design inference:** original educational/product choices, such as study routines and practice durations. Never badge these as MARA policy.
- **Unsupported:** quota/threshold/fee/algorithm assertions without applicable primary support. Omit from official-fact UI until verified; keep research leads in notes.

## Revalidation workflow

Before publishing official-fact guidance, attach claim ID/source/applicability/access date, check the current live portal and confirm the source has not been superseded. Recheck admission content each intake and syllabus content each selected examination year. Record contradictions instead of silently replacing inconvenient evidence. Do not access an applicant's private records or enter identity numbers as part of research. Preserve the supplied Gemini dossier unchanged as a secondary lead and its earlier review as a historical session record.
