# Homepage MRSM IGCSE explainer: fact check and approved copy

Checked: 15 November 2026. Scope: a brief public-facing educational explainer and general preparation advice. Research and local application implementation are authorised. No application or root-document files were edited for this research task.

## Evidence boundaries

Re-opened the current official MARA MyMRSM 2027 requirements, its programme PDF and Cambridge's overview/assessment/university guidance. Searched official MARA sources for the specific entry-test claims. A failure to find a public specification means **not independently verified**, not that a claim has been disproved. Do not infer an admission test's rules from a final IGCSE paper or from the separate PKSK/UKKM tables.

No personal learner name, candidate ID, invitation details, venue or individual schedule is recorded here. The homepage must not publish such data. An individual invitation may support private family logistics but cannot establish universal examination format or every applicant's date.

## Claims and dispositions

| Assertion supplied | Finding | Safe publication decision |
|---|---|---|
| Ujian Khas IGCSE is 100% English | No current applicable primary paper specification found. MARA confirms programme-specific selection but does not give this detail in the inspected public sources. | Do not present the percentage as verified. Say English practice is useful general preparation, and follow the actual invitation. |
| Entry test consists of English, Mathematics and Science | The public documents distinguish PKSK, UKKM and special programmes. Their listed PKSK/UKKM components are not proof of IGCSE Ujian Khas content. | Preserve all three practice banks, clearly labelled independent academic preparation. Do not call them the complete official entry paper. |
| A–D multiple choice plus possible subjective questions | No complete current IGCSE special-test format established by inspected primary evidence. Final Cambridge subject papers vary. | Omit A–D/subjective format as an official claim. The platform may provide supported question types as its own practice formats. |
| HOTS rather than memorisation | Cambridge's general assessment description includes subject knowledge, conceptual understanding **and** higher-order thinking. This is not a published IGCSE entry-test blueprint. | Encourage understanding/application without saying memory is irrelevant or SPM is rote-only. |
| MRSM programme combines KSSM and Cambridge | Supported by the programme PDF's IGCSE description, which also includes MRSM's curriculum. | State the combination with source/year qualifier. |
| IGCSE Form 4, SPM Form 5 | Supported by the programme PDF's current IGCSE description. It does not establish identical series/dates/subjects for every cohort. | Use “Panduan MARA menyatakan...” and direct families to the maktab for details. |
| All IGCSE subjects taught/tested 100% English | Too broad. Cambridge describes English-medium assessment generally, but this does not prove every language subject or classroom practice, nor all MRSM instruction. | Avoid the absolute claim. Encourage academic English and check actual subject/language arrangements. |
| IGCSE is “international SPM” | An accessible analogy can conceal distinct qualification/assessment structures. Cambridge aligns IGCSE standards with GCSE in England, while MARA describes separate IGCSE and SPM examinations. | Say “kelayakan menengah antarabangsa Cambridge”, not interchangeable or automatically equivalent to SPM in all uses. |
| A global-university ticket | Cambridge identifies recognition and preparation for further study; a university admission decision has its own criteria, often requiring later qualifications. | Do not promise direct admission, guaranteed offers, scholarships or automatic superiority. |
| The child's invitation fixes a date/venue | A personal logistics claim is not verified by these public sources and does not generalise to others. | Keep personal schedule/venue/name out of public homepage content. Refer privately to the original invitation when needed. |

## Four concise homepage cards

Each sourced card should expose a readable official-source link, not only a decorative “verified” label. The MARA content is qualified to the 2027-source check and should be reviewed each intake.

### 1. Apa itu MRSM IGCSE?

Program ini menggabungkan KSSM, kurikulum khas MRSM dan Cambridge IGCSE.

Source: [Panduan program MARA, halaman 8](https://mrsm.mara.gov.my/mymrsm/Syarat%20dan%20Pilihan%20Program%20MRSM.pdf).

### 2. IGCSE dan SPM

Panduan MARA menyatakan IGCSE pada akhir Tingkatan 4 dan SPM semasa Tingkatan 5. Semak susunan subjek dengan pihak maktab.

Source: [Panduan program MARA, halaman 8](https://mrsm.mara.gov.my/mymrsm/Syarat%20dan%20Pilihan%20Program%20MRSM.pdf).

### 3. Fahami dan gunakan ilmu

Cambridge menilai pengetahuan, kefahaman konsep dan kemahiran berfikir aras tinggi. Biasakan diri menerangkan sebab serta langkah jawapan.

Source: [Cambridge: Assessment](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/why-choose-us/benefits-of-a-cambridge-education/assessment/). The second sentence is general preparation advice, not a quoted Cambridge requirement.

### 4. Semak ujian khas anda

Bagi pengambilan 2027, calon program khas yang memenuhi syarat merit perlu menduduki ujian khas MARA. Ikut arahan rasmi untuk format, masa dan persediaan.

Source: [Syarat kemasukan MyMRSM 2027](https://mrsm.mara.gov.my/mymrsm/). The exact conditions and first-choice programme requirements remain in the linked source. Do not imply that every applicant receives an invitation or that admission is guaranteed.

Suggested short footer:

“Latihan bebas, bukan kertas rasmi MARA atau Cambridge. Format sebenar ujian khas perlu dirujuk pada arahan rasmi. Maklumat disemak pada 15 November 2026.”

## Practical preparation bullets

Label these **Cadangan persediaan**, not “format rasmi” or proven admission-success advice:

- Baca petikan pendek dalam Bahasa Inggeris dan ceritakan maksudnya dengan ayat sendiri.
- Fahami kata arahan seperti calculate, compare dan explain; semak maksudnya mengikut konteks soalan.
- Untuk Matematik, tulis langkah, semak unit dan buat anggaran sebelum memilih jawapan.
- Untuk Sains, kenal pasti apa yang diubah, diukur dan dikekalkan dalam sesuatu penyiasatan.
- Mulakan dengan set pendek tanpa pemasa, kemudian cuba latihan masa secara beransur.
- Semak penerangan bagi kesilapan; ulang topik yang belum jelas dan berehat apabila perlu.
- Baca arahan pada jemputan rasmi bersama ibu bapa; jangan membuat andaian tentang alat, tempat atau masa ujian.

These suggestions are original general educational guidance. Do not represent the app's timings, difficulty labels or results as MARA selection standards.
