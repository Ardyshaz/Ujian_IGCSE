# Cambridge IGCSE: curriculum and assessment research

Research date: 15 November 2026. Implementation is authorised; official entrance-format uncertainty must remain visible. This is a research baseline, not a claim that a Year 6 pupil is studying a complete IGCSE programme. Source metadata and claim statuses are indexed in [07-sources-and-verification.md](07-sources-and-verification.md).

## Stages, curriculum and qualifications

**C-101 — verified overview:** Cambridge describes IGCSE as a qualification typically for ages 14–16, offering over 70 subjects and flexible combinations. Assessment may involve written, oral, coursework and practical components, depending on subject. This does not mean every subject uses every component. [Cambridge IGCSE overview, S-C01](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-upper-secondary/cambridge-igcse/).

**C-102 — verified stage distinction:** Cambridge Lower Secondary typically addresses ages 11–14 and offers optional assessments. Treat this stage as a bridge distinct from final IGCSE examinations. Its optional-assessment description does not establish which assessments a particular MRSM cohort must take. [Lower Secondary, S-C02](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-lower-secondary/).

**Design inference:** The product should label primary foundations, lower-secondary bridge and IGCSE extension separately. Grammar, fractions, scientific observation and language confidence suit preparation without claiming to replicate a complete admissions paper. Do not infer age eligibility for MRSM from Cambridge's typical ages.

## Official reference resources and access limits

**C-111 — verified resource availability:** Cambridge provides syllabuses, specimen/past papers, mark schemes, examiner reports, learner guides and example candidate responses through subject pages and its secure School Support Hub; some resources need registered-school access. Public papers may describe older syllabuses. Link rather than mirror protected material. [Classroom support, S-C11](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-upper-secondary/cambridge-igcse/classroom-support/), [Study resources, S-C12](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/why-choose-us/parents-and-students/learning-with-lasting-impact/study-resources/).

Resource investigation checklist:

- Match subject code, year, tier and component before examining format/objectives.
- Use specimen questions to understand assessment structure without copying questions or mark schemes into the original bank.
- Use examiner commentary to identify misconceptions; author new distractors and explanations.
- Record inaccessible teacher materials as unavailable. Do not bypass school login or claim to have read restricted content.
- Sample publicly accessible English resources are listed on [0510 past papers, S-C13](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-igcse-english-second-language-oral-endorsement-0510/past-papers/).

## Comparison and remaining evidence

KSSM is the national secondary curriculum described in MARA's programme material, while Cambridge supplies its own syllabus/assessment specifications. Neither acronym alone establishes a universal teaching language, difficulty advantage or examination equivalence. Avoid claims that one system is rote-only and the other uniformly analytical. Admission recognition and university entry conditions are institution-specific; this app cannot guarantee scholarships or offers.
