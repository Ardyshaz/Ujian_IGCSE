# UKKM, PKSK and programme-specific selection

Research/access date: 15 November 2026. Applicability: 2027 intake sources, subject to changes and the child's actual invitation. This app provides independent practice, not an official exam or selection algorithm.

## Supported distinctions

**A-401 — verified route distinction:** The current [MARA portal, S-M01](https://mrsm.mara.gov.my/mymrsm/) differentiates PKSK/UKKM and requires qualifying applicants to specified special programmes, including IGCSE, to take the MARA special assessment. It also states a UKKM condition for qualifying Premier-first-choice applicants. Therefore “all MRSM candidates sit one identical UKKM” is not a safe simplification.

**A-402 — verified listed learning components:** MARA's [programme document, S-M03](https://mrsm.mara.gov.my/mymrsm/Syarat%20dan%20Pilihan%20Program%20MRSM.pdf), page 15, lists UKKM Bahasa Melayu, Mathematics, Science and personality. It lists IGCSE among special programmes on that page. Listing a programme does not establish its test's exact content or timing.

**A-403 — qualified application detail:** The [MARA 2027 FAQ, S-M02](https://mrsm.mara.gov.my/mymrsm/FAQ%20MYMRSM2026.pdf) discusses first-choice special programmes and merit-qualified invitations. It does not supply a complete public paper specification here. The actual child's programme invitation remains decisive for logistics.

## Boundaries for the simulation

| Platform activity | What it can honestly claim | What it cannot claim |
|---|---|---|
| English/Mathematics/Science quiz | Original practice in selected skills | Complete official MRSM selection coverage |
| Examination Mode | Timed independent practice with saved results | Exact official paper, weighting, pass threshold or admission prediction |
| Challenge Mode | More demanding questions and configurable practice time pressure | Proof of superior intelligence or boarding-school suitability |
| Daily Challenge | A short learning habit across subjects | Official admission ranking |
| Student/parent advice | Source-linked route explanations and general preparation | Guaranteed programme placement, automatic fallback or scholarship |

These restrictions are product interpretation and honest labelling, not official examination claims.

## Preparation decisions

Keep all three core banks. Expand research toward Bahasa Melayu, reasoning, general knowledge and writing where the applicable assessment calls for them; do not remove English because it is absent from one UKKM subject list. A programme-specific test and the wider curriculum may have distinct needs.

Practice needs stable timers, question navigation and review flags, but product defaults must be labelled as practice settings. Do not use the dossier's 20/30/80 question counts, 30-minute component timings, 35-minute special tests, calculator restrictions or 60% minimum as current verified exam constants. Such statements need current applicable official evidence.

Personality-related guidance should encourage honest answers and constructive reflection. Do not author a supposedly correct personality-answer bank, diagnose psychological resilience or collect sensitive mental-health information. The academic progress dashboard must not suggest it measures psychological fitness.

## Outstanding evidence

Exact question counts, subject distribution, language, duration, delivery mode, calculator/material rules, interview requirements, thresholds and exam-day procedures remain unverified for the child's specific invitation. The 18 November 2026 parent-provided date remains context only. Recheck the official invitation and current MARA assessment notices; do not enter personal identity numbers in research tools.
