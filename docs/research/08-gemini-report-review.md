## Primary-source spot checks

All access dates below are 15 November 2026. Dates shown inside a source describe that source; they are not independently authenticated publication timestamps.

### S-01 — Cambridge Mathematics 0580, examinations 2025–2027

Publisher: Cambridge University Press & Assessment / Cambridge International. Version 3, stated published May 2024. Official URL: [Mathematics 0580 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/662466-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf).

Checked syllabus pages 9 and 69–70. Core entry uses Papers 1/3 and grades C–G; Extended entry uses Papers 2/4 and grades A*–E. Papers 1/2 prohibit calculators and Papers 3/4 require scientific calculators. The change list removes Extended linear programming/box-and-whisker plots and adds domain/range and exact trigonometric values. These observations support some dossier details but do not support attaching 9–1 grades to code 0580. Check the separate numerical-grade qualification before making any equivalence statement. None of these paper rules establishes a Year 6 admission test format.

### S-02 — Cambridge First Language English 0500, examinations 2027–2029

Publisher: Cambridge University Press & Assessment / Cambridge International. Version 1; copyright September 2024, publication/update date not otherwise established here. Official URL: [First Language English 0500 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/718783-2027-2029-syllabus.pdf).

Checked syllabus pages 13–16 and the changes section. Paper 1 is two hours/80 marks with four compulsory 20-mark questions on three texts. Paper 2 Question 1(a) has five reading marks targeting R3/R4, so the dossier's description as a test of attitude and explicit meaning is imprecise. The paper structure is corroborated, but claims about cognitive fatigue or why universities prefer it are not established by this syllabus. This is a specific First Language route, not proof of the route used by a particular MRSM cohort or an entrance English test.

### S-03 — Cambridge Co-ordinated Sciences 0654, examinations 2025–2027

Publisher: Cambridge University Press & Assessment / Cambridge International. Version 1; publication/update date not established in this review. Official URL: [Co-ordinated Sciences 0654 syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/664572-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf).

Checked syllabus pages 10, 79–80 and 105. Weighting is 30% multiple choice, 50% theory and 20% practical assessment. The qualification grade range includes A*A* as its highest grade, so the report's AA–GG description omits the top grade. Core eligibility is CC–GG; Extended eligibility is A*A*–GG. The paper pattern is corroborated for this syllabus period only; it is not evidence that every MRSM IGCSE student takes 0654, nor that full IGCSE laboratory content is appropriate for all Year 6 practice.

### S-04 — MARA English-language MRSM FAQ cited by the dossier

Publisher: Majlis Amanah Rakyat. Official URL: [MRSM FAQ](https://www.mara.gov.my/en/index/faq/frequently-asked-questions-faq-mrsm/). Publication/update date not established; explicit applicability is “MRSM INTAKE FOR 2020”.

The page discusses 2019 applications and the 2020 intake. Its presence on an official domain does not make those rules current for 2026/2027. Treat it as historical context and replace current-intake eligibility/procedure claims with applicable notices. This check confirms the applicability problem, not current citizenship, health, blacklist, assessment or offer rules.

### S-05 — KPM FAQ for school-specific and MRSM applications, labelled 2027

Publisher: Kementerian Pendidikan Malaysia. [Official landing page](https://www.moe.gov.my/soalan-lazim-faq-pksk-tahun-2027), linking to [FAQ PDF](https://www.moe.gov.my/storage/files/shares/Soalan%20Lazim%20FAQ/FAQ%20PKSK%202027.pdf), nine pages.

Questions 2–6 describe a shared application system and distinguish pupils by MOEIS/APDM registration, with MyMRSM for unregistered pupils. This is more precise than classifying all applicants solely by government/private school labels. However, the retrieved PDF footer says “Kemaskini Julai 2027”, later than this review date, while the landing page's generic update footer is November 2026. Record this date inconsistency and corroborate version/intake applicability before publishing dates or rules. The FAQ discusses PKSK; it does not independently settle UKKM obligations for every applicant or specialised test. Do not infer absence of UKKM merely because that term is absent here.
