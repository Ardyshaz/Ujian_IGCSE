# Bilingual exam-language glossary expansion

Updated 15 November 2026 following the parent's request. The glossary now contains **100 unique terms**: 21 command words, 33 Mathematics, 28 Science, 15 English language and 3 general exam-phrasing entries. The original 32 entries and all parent-proposed additions are retained, with contextual corrections. Entries are simple learning explanations and editorial Malay equivalents, not an official exhaustive admission word list.

## Content choices and corrections

- Include the proposed state/suggest/predict/summarise/justify/determine/contrast and technical subject vocabulary.
- Add practical word-problem cues (sum, total, each, remaining, at least/at most) and reading cues (except, most likely, refer to).
- Avoid narrowing respiration to oxygen use: distinguish aerobic and anaerobic processes. Avoid defining every solution solely as a solid dissolved in liquid; explain solute/solvent with an age-appropriate example.
- Distinguish line and rotational symmetry; explain that adverbs can modify more than verbs. Use “Nyatakan secara ringkas” for outline so it is not mistaken for underlining.
- State that translations and command meanings depend on question/subject context. Do not guarantee a term appears in the child's actual assessment.
